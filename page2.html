For you

There are nights when I miss you so much it aches. I stare at the ceiling and imagine what it would feel like to fall asleep beside you, to hold your hand, to hear your laughter in person. But even in this distance, even in all the silence between our calls, my love for you stays strong. We may not get to share physical moments right now, but we share something even deeper — faith, commitment, and a love that refuses to fade. Every message from you lights up my day, and every little conversation keeps me going. I know it’s not always easy, but I believe in us. I believe in the day we’ll close this distance and I’ll finally get to look into your eyes, not through a screen, but for real — and tell you, “We made it.” Until then, I’m here. I’m loving you from afar, every second of every day. Forever yours,
